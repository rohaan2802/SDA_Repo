Think of your project group as a newly established company consisting of three members. You are required to submit a PDF document that includes the following details:

1. Team Members: List the team members along with their names, roll numbers, and roles. (Team Manager, Analyst, Designer, Developer, QA Manager)
2. Company Information:
Provide the following:
Company Name
Company Logo
Submission Guidelines:
Only one submission per group is required.
Cross-section groups are not allowed.
File Naming Convention: Use the format: GroupName_SectionName.pdf where GroupName is your chosen company name.
Note: If you do not follow the naming convention, marks will be deducted.
